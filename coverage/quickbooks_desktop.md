# Coverage: QuickBooks Desktop → ERPNext

**Out of scope, by operator decision (2026-10-21):** QuickBooks is web only. There is no QuickBooks Desktop
adapter, connection option or Web Connector / qbXML route, and none is planned. A business on QuickBooks
Desktop migrates its company file to QuickBooks Online (Intuit's own Desktop-to-Online migration) and
connects that instead: see [quickbooks_online.md](quickbooks_online.md).
